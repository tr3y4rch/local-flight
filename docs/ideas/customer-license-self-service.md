# Customer license self-service — pending product approval

Existing source includes a customer Relay Access recovery page and holder actions
for key resend/replacement and device changes. The page is published, but its
presence does not establish approval, release readiness, or a certified customer
journey. The email identity/branding change does not implement or expand this tool.

Before promoting self-service management in emails or expanding these features,
obtain explicit product approval for the customer journey and wording. Confirm
which actions belong on the website versus support or the app, then certify the
complete journey with real mailbox links and the required licensing readiness.
Preserve explicit confirmation for destructive actions, verified-email ownership,
rate limits, and the distinction between key resend and key replacement.

Until separately approved, ordinary license and device-change emails direct
customers to Beacon Tools support. Existing one-time verification/recovery links
remain supported, without advertising a broader customer dashboard.
