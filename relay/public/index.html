It is not a data guarantee.

Upstream aviation data can still be delayed, cached, rate-limited, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable. Local Flight shows freshness in the app.

The privacy boundary stays narrow.

Remote Companion messages remain end-to-end encrypted. The encryption key stays on the paired devices, so Beacon Tools cannot read the request or response.

Notice / informational only

For display and hobby use only. Local Flight and relay data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, wrong, or unavailable. Do not use them for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.
