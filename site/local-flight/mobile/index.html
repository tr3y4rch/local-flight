Two setup paths

Pair with your own server, or use phone-only mode.

Companion mode

Pairs with your Local Flight desktop app or Raspberry Pi over your own Wi-Fi/LAN. Scan the QR code from Settings, or enter the LAN address manually with the :8000 port. The paired server provides the board, radar, history, controls, reports, and LED matrix tools.

Phone-only mode

Connects directly to the Beacon Tools relay for a simplified board, radar, weather, history, settings, and reports. Your desktop or Raspberry Pi does not need to be online.

Board

Airport header, weather strip, pinned flight, compact rows, local/UTC time, and passenger-friendly status labels.

Radar

Nearby traffic with range rings. Data comes from your paired server or the shared relay, depending on setup mode.

History

Companion mode reads your server history. Phone-only mode keeps recent movement history locally on the phone.

Reports

Manual reports are always user-triggered. Automatic crash diagnostics only run when your mobile diagnostics choice allows them.

Phone-only limits

Phone-only mode updates more carefully.

The shared relay is intentionally conservative so public testing stays sustainable.

  • Phone-only board updates are designed around a 3-hour cadence rather than rapid polling.
  • Phone-only radar updates are designed around a 5-minute cadence while visible.
  • Phone-only radar ranges are limited to 1, 3, 5, and 10 nautical miles.
  • Phone-only mode does not include LED matrix controls, server maintenance controls, or LAN control panels.

Not for professional or safety-critical aviation use

Local Flight Mobile is an informational display aid only. Flight, radar, weather, map, and relay data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, wrong, or unavailable. Do not use it for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.

Privacy model

No accounts, no ads SDKs, and no cross-app tracking. Companion mode talks to your own server first. Phone-only mode uses the hosted relay and keeps its own history locally on the phone. See privacy policy and privacy choices.
