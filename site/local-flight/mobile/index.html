Two setup paths

Companion when you have a server. Standalone when you do not.

LAN Companion

Pairs with your Local Flight desktop or Raspberry Pi server over your own Wi-Fi/LAN. Pair from the native or LAN browser Settings QR, or enter the LAN URL manually with the :8000 port. It uses the paired server for Board, Radar, History, Control, Help, WebSocket updates, reports, and safe Matrix controls.

Standalone

Talks directly to the Beacon Tools relay for a simplified mobile board, radar, METAR, history, settings, and reports. It does not need your desktop or Pi server online.

Board

Airport header, METAR strip, pinned flight, compact rows, local/UTC time, and passenger-friendly status labels.

Radar

Mobile-owned range rings with LAN or relay-backed aircraft data, depending on setup mode.

History

LAN Companion reads server history. Standalone keeps deduped movement history locally on the phone.

Reports

Manual reports are user-triggered. Automatic crash diagnostics only run when the mobile diagnostics choice allows them.

Platform previews

Same app shape, tuned for each screen.

Preview screenshots are split between iOS and Android so store metadata and product pages can show the real mobile surfaces directly.

Relay limits

Standalone is intentionally slower and smaller.

The hosted relay is shared infrastructure, so mobile Standalone protects it with conservative refresh and radar limits.

  • Standalone FIDS refresh is designed around a 3-hour policy rather than rapid polling.
  • Standalone radar refresh is designed around a 5-minute policy while visible.
  • Standalone radar ranges are limited to 1, 3, 5, and 10 nautical miles.
  • Standalone does not expose Matrix, admin, scheduler restart, server URL tools, or LAN control panels.

Safety disclaimer

Local Flight Mobile is an informational display aid. Flight, radar, weather, and surface data can be delayed, incomplete, cached, wrong, or unavailable. Do not use it for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, or safety decisions.

Privacy model

No accounts, no ads SDKs, and no cross-app tracking. LAN Companion talks to your server first. Standalone uses the hosted relay and stores its own history locally on the phone. See privacy policy and privacy choices.
