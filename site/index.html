Privacy policy

Not for professional or safety-critical aviation use

Local Flight, Beacon Tools pages, screenshots, and relay data are informational display aids only. Data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, wrong, or unavailable. Do not use them for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.
