Your airport board, wherever you are.

Local Flight brings a focused flight board to your iPhone, iPad, and compatible Apple-silicon Mac. Pair on the same Wi-Fi with a Local Flight host on macOS, Windows, Linux, or Raspberry Pi to see arrivals, departures, nearby traffic, movement history, and relevant host controls.

Choose “Connect to a Local Flight host” during setup. This becomes Companion mode and connects directly to your host nearby. If you explicitly enable Remote Companion, the same paired device can continue through end-to-end encrypted relay routing away from home, as long as your host remains online. The nearby connection is always preferred, and remote access can be revoked from the host.

NO HOST? USE STANDALONE.

Choose “Use without a Local Flight host” for a simpler airport board through the Beacon Tools service. This becomes Standalone mode. Choose an airport and start browsing Airline schedules or VATSIM traffic without running Local Flight elsewhere. Careful refresh limits keep shared aviation-data access reliable and fairly available.

BUILT FOR DAILY GLANCES

• Arrivals and departures with airport-local times
• Status, delay, terminal, and gate details when supplied by the selected data source
• A responsive radar-style view of nearby aircraft
• Deduplicated movement history that avoids counting repeated snapshots and known codeshares as separate flights
• Flight detail sheets using the data available for each movement
• Pinned-flight and airport-board Home Screen widgets using the latest snapshot prepared by the app
• A best-effort pinned-flight Live Activity on supported iPhones, started only when you ask for it
• Warm light, midnight dark, device-matched, and high-contrast appearances
• Adaptive Board / Radar / History / More navigation for iPhone and iPad
• An explicit fullscreen Display with the familiar horizontal FIDS columns

FOR AVIATION ENTHUSIASTS

Local Flight keeps the technical depth close without forcing it into every screen. Companion can reflect real-flight sources configured on your host, or a VATSIM-focused virtual view with callsigns, aircraft, filed routes, altitude, speed, and network freshness when available.

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

• No Local Flight account required
• No advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking
• Companion talks to your own server first
• Remote Companion traffic is end-to-end encrypted
• Standalone movement history stays on your device
• Automatic diagnostics are an explicit choice
• Camera access is used only for optional pairing QR codes; manual entry remains available

OPTIONAL SUPPORT

Three one-time App Store support choices are available in More. They unlock nothing, add no subscription, and do not change app features. Apple handles payment details; Local Flight receives only the minimal transaction evidence needed for verification.

WHAT YOU NEED

Companion requires a Local Flight desktop, Linux server, or Raspberry Pi host. Remote Companion additionally requires explicit pairing, relay access, and an online host.

Standalone requires internet access and intentionally refreshes less frequently than Companion.

IMPORTANT

Local Flight is an informational display aid. Flight, weather, radar, airport-surface, and relay data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, incorrect, or unavailable.

Do not use Local Flight for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.
