Your airport board, wherever you are.

Local Flight brings a focused flight board to your iPhone and iPad. Pair on the same Wi-Fi with a Local Flight host on macOS, Windows, Linux, or Raspberry Pi to see arrivals, departures, nearby traffic, and movement history.

Choose “Connect to a Local Flight host” during setup. This becomes Companion mode and connects directly to your host nearby. If you explicitly enable Remote Companion, the same paired device can continue through end-to-end encrypted relay routing away from home, as long as your host remains online. The nearby connection is always preferred, and remote access can be revoked from the host.

NO HOST? USE STANDALONE.

Choose “Use without a Local Flight host” for a simpler airport board. This becomes Standalone mode. The app is free. VATSIM virtual traffic is free; real airline schedules use the optional annual Relay Access subscription. Relay Access can be active on one phone using real-flight Standalone or one Local Flight desktop/Pi host. Restore checks existing annual or eligible founder access before offering a purchase, and Mobile never asks for or displays an LFRA key during setup.

BUILT FOR DAILY GLANCES

• Arrivals and departures with airport-local times
• Status, delay, terminal, and gate details when supplied by the selected data source
• A responsive radar-style view through Relay Access, Companion, or VATSIM
• Deduplicated movement history
• Flight detail sheets using available movement data
• Pinned-flight and airport-board Home Screen widgets
• A best-effort pinned-flight Live Activity on supported iPhones, started only when you ask for it
• Warm light, midnight dark, device-matched, and high-contrast appearances
• Adaptive Board / Radar / History / More navigation for iPhone and iPad
• An explicit fullscreen Display with the familiar horizontal FIDS columns

FOR AVIATION ENTHUSIASTS

Companion can reflect real-flight sources configured on your host, or a VATSIM-focused virtual view with callsigns, aircraft, filed routes, altitude, speed, and network freshness when available.

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

• No Local Flight account required
• No advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking
• Companion talks to your own server first
• Remote Companion traffic is end-to-end encrypted
• Standalone movement history stays on your device
• Automatic diagnostics are an explicit choice
• Companion follows your Local Flight host and does not use another main-device place
• Email stays optional and appears only if you later choose protection or recovery
• Camera access is used only for optional pairing QR codes; manual entry remains available

OPTIONAL SUPPORT

Three one-time App Store support choices are available in More. They unlock nothing, do not extend Relay Access, and do not change app features. Apple handles payment details; Local Flight receives only the minimal transaction evidence needed for verification.

WHAT YOU NEED

Companion requires a Local Flight desktop, Linux server, or Raspberry Pi host. Remote Companion additionally requires explicit pairing, Relay Access on that host, and an online host. Companion follows the host and consumes no additional place. Moving Relay Access to a different main device requires verified ownership and a named confirmation; the previous main device stays active until the receiving device stores and commits its new credential.

Standalone requires internet access and intentionally refreshes less frequently than Companion. Shared real-aircraft radar is included in Relay Access on a licensed monthly allowance, suiting regular viewing rather than a screen left on radar all day. VATSIM Radar remains available without it.

IMPORTANT

Local Flight is an informational display aid. Flight, weather, radar, airport-surface, and relay data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, incorrect, or unavailable.

Do not use Local Flight for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.
