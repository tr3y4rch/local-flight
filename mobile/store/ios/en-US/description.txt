Your airport board, wherever you are.

Local Flight turns your iPhone or iPad into a focused flight information display (FIDS) companion for your Mac, Windows PC, or Raspberry Pi. Pair on your own Wi-Fi to bring arrivals, departures, nearby traffic, movement history, and host controls to a purpose-built mobile interface.

At home, Companion connects directly to your Local Flight host over the LAN. If you explicitly enable Remote Companion, the same paired phone can continue through end-to-end encrypted relay routing when you leave Wi-Fi, as long as your host remains online. LAN is always preferred, and remote access can be revoked from the host.

NO SERVER? USE STANDALONE.

Standalone provides a simpler phone-only airport board through the Beacon Tools relay. Choose an airport and start browsing without a desktop or Raspberry Pi. Standalone uses careful refresh limits to keep shared aviation-data access reliable and fairly available.

BUILT FOR DAILY GLANCES

• Arrivals and departures with airport-local times
• Status, delay, terminal, and gate details when supplied by the selected data source
• A responsive radar-style view of nearby aircraft
• Deduplicated movement history that avoids counting repeated snapshots and known codeshares as separate flights
• Flight detail sheets using the data available for each movement
• Pinned-flight and airport-board Home Screen widgets using the latest snapshot prepared by the app
• Light and dark appearances with multiple display skins
• Portrait and landscape layouts for iPhone and iPad

FOR AVIATION ENTHUSIASTS

Local Flight keeps the technical depth close without forcing it into every screen. Companion can reflect real-flight sources configured on your host, or a VATSIM-focused virtual view with callsigns, aircraft, filed routes, altitude, speed, and network freshness when available.

LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

• No Local Flight account required
• No advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking
• Companion talks to your own server first
• Remote Companion traffic is end-to-end encrypted
• Standalone movement history stays on your device
• Automatic diagnostics are an explicit choice
• Camera access is used only for optional pairing QR codes; manual entry remains available

OPTIONAL SUPPORT

Three one-time App Store support choices are available in Settings. They unlock nothing, add no subscription, and do not change app features. Apple handles payment details; Local Flight receives only the minimal transaction evidence needed for verification.

WHAT YOU NEED

Companion requires a Local Flight desktop or Raspberry Pi host. Remote Companion additionally requires explicit pairing, relay access, and an online host.

Standalone requires internet access and intentionally refreshes less frequently than Companion.

IMPORTANT

Local Flight is an informational display aid. Flight, weather, radar, airport-surface, and relay data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, incorrect, or unavailable.

Do not use Local Flight for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.
